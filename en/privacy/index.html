Privacy policy

Effective: November 9, 2026

1. Information we process

The site has no accounts. We receive the following only when you send a message through the contact form.

  • Required: name, email, topic, subject, message
  • Optional: book, practice setup (file version, chapter, OS, error message)

2. Purpose

To read and answer your message. Reports about practice files and errata are also used to fix the books and files. It is not used for anything else.

3. Retention and deletion

Kept for one year after the reply, then deleted without delay so it cannot be recovered. If a law requires longer retention, it is kept for that period.

4. Where it is stored

Messages pass through Google Apps Script and are stored in the author's Google account (Gmail, and optionally Google Sheets). Google account data may be stored on servers run by Google LLC outside Korea.

5. Sharing

We do not share your information with anyone else.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete, or stop the processing of the information you sent. Send the request through the contact form or to the email below.

7. Browser storage and outside services

The site sets no cookies; it keeps only your language choice in browser storage (localStorage). When YouTube videos on book pages or the web font (jsDelivr) load, those services may receive connection data such as your IP address.
