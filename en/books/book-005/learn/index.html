Final mission run On YouTube

Here is the book's final mission, run start to finish. One key press sends the robot to the guide point, where it opens its screen, folds it again, and drives the same route back. The QR code in Chapter 25 links to this video.

Watch on YouTube ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Permanent QR address in the book: ronniekimbooks.com/en/books/book-005/v/C01-25-00/

What to watch

  • In the turning step, the wheels change angle first and the body rotates a moment later
  • The return drive starting while the screen is still folding
  • The console adding one line per step (the book shows how to use those lines to find where a run stopped)

This is a single recorded run; it is not a benchmark for completion time or arrival accuracy.

Eight motions, one key at a time On YouTube

The book shows these motions as still pictures. Here they play at full speed, one short clip per key. Each card below says what to look for.

Watch on YouTube ↗ (opens in a new tab)

WForwardThe moment all four wheels start together, and how far the body slides after you let go
SBackwardThe reverse of W. Does it start and stop the same way?
ATurn leftThe wheels stay straight, so how does the body turn left?
DTurn rightIs it simply A mirrored? Where is the center of the turn?
ZRotate leftThe wheels angle first, and only then does the body rotate
CRotate rightHow the wheel posture mirrors Z
QDiagonal leftAll four wheels tilt the same way; the body keeps facing forward while moving at an angle
EDiagonal rightLike Q. Watch when the screen switches between STEERING and READY

A and Z both turn the body left, yet the wheel posture is completely different. The book explains what makes the difference with a top view of all four wheels.
