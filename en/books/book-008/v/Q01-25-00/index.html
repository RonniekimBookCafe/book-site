About this video

This is the address where the video of QUAD-01's final walking mission will be published.

Back on the same WALK model and keys as Chapter 1, the robot stands at the start line, walks forward, turns, walks again, and holds its stop pose in the target zone.

The mission is run several times under the same conditions, recording whether the robot walked the whole way, stayed on its feet, and stopped in the zone; then one problem is picked, one value changed, and the runs compared again.

Keys in this exercise

KeyAction
WWalk forward (while held)
A / DTurn left / right in place
XStop pose in the target zone
0Relax, then end the script

This mission isn't autonomous walking with sensors judging the destination; a person presses the keys in sequence and judges success by watching the screen. The mission card and checklist are in Appendix D of the book.

Mission sequence

  1. Stand
  2. Walk
  3. Turn
  4. Walk again
  5. Stop in the zone
