About this video

A full run of LEG3, combining the quadruped's four legs to check joint motions and stepping order in RoboCo on Steam.

Control of single joints and foot positions is extended to all four legs.

After checking the front-left leg (FL) from basic moves through extreme poses, reach, and joint limits, it tries lifting one leg at a time, diagonal legs, a three-leg stance, and a stepping sequence.

Keys in this exercise

F1Key help

Tests 1–5 check joints, IK, and reach; tests 6–9 check how the four legs work together.

Once joint reach and motion are confirmed, those results are tied into actual gait control.

What to watch

  • The other three legs holding the body while one lifts
  • The order in which two diagonal legs move as a pair
  • The robot skipping a command when a target is out of reach
