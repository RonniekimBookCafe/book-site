About this video

A full run of BALANCE: the quadruped uses its IMU and PID control to correct its balance on a tilting test platform in RoboCo on Steam.

The three-axis platform changes roll, pitch, and yaw, and the robot computes correction angles from the attitude its IMU measures.

It compares balance correction off and on, and changes the PID gains KP, KI, and KD to show how the control response differs.

BALANCE uses two Microcontrollers. The robot's joints and sensors and the platform's servos are run by separate Python scripts.

Control structure

  1. Platform tilt
  2. IMU reading
  3. Filtering
  4. PID correction
  5. New joint targets
  6. Posture response

What to watch

  • How far the body tilts with balance off versus on
  • How quickly the body levels out after the platform stops
  • What the console logs when KP, KI, and KD change
