About this video

A full run of LEG1, controlling the quadruped's three joints (HX, HY, KN) one at a time in RoboCo on Steam.

It shows the three-degree-of-freedom structure of one leg and moves the forward/back, side-to-side, and knee joints separately.

Selecting each of the FL, FR, HL, and HR legs compares the joints' turning directions, and automatic tests check each leg's motion and left-right symmetry.

Keys in this exercise

KeyAction
W / SHY joint forward / back
A / DHX joint left / right
E / QKN joint bend / extend
U / O / J / LSelect leg FL / FR / HL / HR
KMove the selected leg's joints to 90°
1~4Automatic joint test per leg
5Spread HX on all four legs
6Pose sweep with joint combinations
0Joint zero pose
F1Key help
F2Back to the stand pose
F3Stop the automatic test

In LEG1, key 0 is the joint zero pose, which moves every joint to 90°. It's a different state from the standing pose you get with F2.

What to watch

  • Which way HX, HY, and KN each turn
  • A left leg and a right leg looking mirrored on the same key
  • How the Port number on screen pairs with the joint that moved
