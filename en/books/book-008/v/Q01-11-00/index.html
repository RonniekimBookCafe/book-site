About this video

A full run of LEG2: changing the quadruped's foot target coordinates and computing joint angles with inverse kinematics (IK) in RoboCo on Steam.

When you set a foot target as x·y·z coordinates, the author's Python library computes the IK and sends the resulting joint angles to RoboCo's ServoMotors.

The selected leg's foot moves forward/back, left/right, and up/down, then follows a circular path, showing how foot position relates to joint angles.

The FK error on screen is the difference between the target and the position computed back from the joint angles. It isn't a separate measurement of where the foot actually is.

The calculation chain on screen

  1. Foot target
  2. IK
  3. HX · HY · KN angles
  4. Servo targets
  5. Compare with FK

What to watch

  • All three joints moving together when the target changes
  • The IK angles and the angles sent to the servos being different numbers
  • The path the foot traces in the circle test
