Sample assessment criteria

Assess motion and explanation, comparison and improvement, not code length.

  1. Did the robot walk the mission card (no falls · stop pose held · inside the target zone)?
  2. Can the learner explain the walk in layers: gait, foot path, IK, joints?
  3. Did they record one changed value and before/after results under the same conditions?
  4. Did they find the first abnormal line in the console log?
  5. Did they fix one condition and rerun at least three times under the same conditions?

Record sheets and the final mission checklist are in Appendix D of the book.
