From the Prologue

Parents today are not struggling because information is scarce. Search brings thousands of educational methods, and new AI advice appears every day. The more answers we hear, the harder it can become to know which choice is right for one particular child.

The book therefore changes the question. Instead of beginning with “Should my child learn coding, English, or AI?”, it asks: Does this choice help my child become a person who can think for themselves?

That question leads to the book’s central idea: an inner compass. It is not a guarantee that a child will never feel lost. It is a standard that helps a child pause, think, and find direction again after being pulled by trends, other people’s expectations, or a polished AI answer.

Key Idea

AI may give the answers, but children must find their own direction in life.

The chapters apply this idea to questions, reading, speaking, source checking, homework, AI tutors, coding, English, career choices, resilience, empathy, ethics, and family AI rules.
